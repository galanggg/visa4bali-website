## Platform

web

## Stack

WordPress + Elementor Pro (hello-elementor theme), WooCommerce for online payment, WP Rocket cache, MetForm, Hostinger. Confirmed by the owner 2026-10-18: any redesign must be buildable on this stack. Home, About Us and Contact Us use the Elementor Canvas template (see audit/TRACKER.md).

## Users

Primary (confirmed 2026-10-18): foreigners — tourists already in Bali or about to arrive, and expats staying longer — who need an Indonesian visa, an extension, or a stay permit. They read English or Russian; testimonials also arrive in Korean. They are usually on a phone, often mid-trip, often against a deadline (visa expiry, overstay risk, flight date).

Secondary (stated in current site copy, not the redesign target): Indonesians travelling abroad.

## Product Purpose

Visa4Bali (PT Visa Empat Bali) handles a client's visa application end to end — documents, submission, immigration liaison, delivery — so the client does not deal with immigration themselves. Success is a visitor starting a case: paying online for a standard visa, or opening a WhatsApp conversation for anything non-standard.

## Positioning

A physical agency in Jimbaran with a named owner (Mr. Rolly), a 20-person named team in uniform, a Lombok branch, and a direct working relationship with immigration rather than brokers. Standard tourist-visa processing 5–6 working days, 3-day expedited option (site copy; verify before quoting).

## Operating Context

Conversion is mixed (confirmed 2026-10-18): standard visas (211/C1, e-VOA, extensions) can be bought through WooCommerce checkout; complex cases (KITAS, family, investor, overstay) go to WhatsApp (+62 878 6227 3133). Office hours Monday–Friday 08:00–17:00. Clients send passport photo and documents by WhatsApp or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services listed on /our-services/: 211 60-day visa, e-VOA 30 days, D1/D2 multiple entry, E33G (remove working visa), C12 & D12, family visa, 211 extension, VOA extension, D1/D2 extension, D12 extension.
- Prices currently live inside Instagram flyer images and in pricing cards; the two disagree in places. Price list is an open item for the owner to confirm before any redesign shows numbers.
- Every page is edited by hand in WP admin; nothing auto-deploys.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: "V4B" wordmark, black with a red "4". No other confirmed brand colours or fonts; the current navy/cyan/orange palette and Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter come from the Elementor template kit, not a brand decision (inferred, unconfirmed).
- Staff uniform: purple batik shirts (seen in the team photography).
- Register (user decision 2026-10-18): trustworthy, mature and professional. The first round of redesign directions (painted roadside signboards, airport arrivals signage, passport ribbon) was rejected as "terlalu anak muda". Design work stays at the familiar, conventional end; trust signals (legal entity, real office, real staff, transparent prices) outrank novelty. A second, conventional round (letterhead, front office, open price list) was "oke tapi kurang sreg"; the user then asked for directions derived from research into leading Bali visa agencies' websites (2026-10-18). Competitor benchmark set: Emerhub, Bali Solve, GetBaliVisa, Visa Agent Bali (VABC), Bali Connect, Let's Move Indonesia, Gaya Bali Visa, Flado.

## Product Principles

1. Time-to-case beats storytelling: a stressed visitor on a phone should reach the right visa and a way to start it in seconds.
2. Real people over stock: the agency's proof is its named team and its office, not generic imagery.
3. Prices and requirements are text, not pictures.
4. One clear route per case type: buy online if standard, talk on WhatsApp if not.

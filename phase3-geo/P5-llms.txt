# Visa4Bali

> Visa agency in Jimbaran, Bali, providing Indonesian tourist visas (211/C1, e-VOA), visa extensions, KITAS stay permits, remote-working (E33G) visas, family visas, and overstay resolution for foreign visitors since 2014. English- and Russian-language support.

## Services

- [Our Services](https://visa4bali.com/our-services/): Full list of visa and extension services with a 4-step process.
- [E-VOA 30 Days](https://visa4bali.com/product/e-voa-30-days/): Electronic Visa on Arrival, 30 days, extendable once to 60 days total, 1–2 working day processing. Rp750,000 (includes government fee).
- [Tourist Visa 211/C1 Extension](https://visa4bali.com/product/social-visa-b-211-ru/): 60-day initial stay, two 60-day extensions, up to 180 days total. Rp1,800,000.
- [Remote Working Visa (E33G)](https://visa4bali.com/product/remote-working-visa-e33g/): 12-month multiple-entry permit for remote workers employed outside Indonesia; income requirement US$60,000/year. Rp12,000,000–13,500,000 (includes US$150 government tax). 5–6 working day processing.
- [Family Visa](https://visa4bali.com/product/family-visa/): Dependent visa for family members of a visa or stay-permit holder.

## FAQ

- Bali tourist visa processing takes 5–6 working days standard, or 3 working days expedited (excludes weekends and Indonesian public holidays).
- Overstaying a valid Indonesian visa or stay permit incurs a fine of IDR 1,000,000 per day; overstays beyond 60 days result in payment of the fine and deportation (Law No. 6 of 2011 on Immigration).

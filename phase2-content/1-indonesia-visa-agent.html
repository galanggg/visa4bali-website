What does a visa agent actually do?

A licensed Indonesian visa agent is far more than a form-filler. The role exists because Indonesia's immigration system is built around local sponsorship and a Bahasa-only online portal that most foreigners cannot navigate safely. A real agent handles:

  • Licensed sponsorship — required for many visa types (B211/C1, KITAS). Your agent is legally accountable for your stay.
  • Document translation & legalization — marriage certificates, bank statements, sponsor letters.
  • Online portal submission — evisa.imigrasi.go.id is Bahasa-only and unforgiving of errors.
  • Biometric appointment scheduling — mandatory in person since 28 May 2025.
  • Office escort & interpretation at Denpasar / Ngurah Rai immigration.
  • Reminder & renewal management — so you never drift into an overstay fine.

The risk of unlicensed agents

Unlicensed "fixers" are cheaper up front and far more expensive later. The common failure modes:

  • Passports held as leverage, or simply lost.
  • Fake or invalid sponsor letters — which can lead to a rejected extension or a deportation order.
  • No physical office and no accountability when something goes wrong.

How to verify any agent (including us)

  • Ask for the immigration sponsor ID.
  • Confirm a physical operating address you can visit.
  • Check years in operation and independent reviews.

Questions clients ask

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp.

What do I need to send for a tourist visa?

A photo of your passport's main page, valid for at least 6 months, and a photo of you holding your passport. Send them by WhatsApp or email.

How long does it take?

It depends on the visa: from 3–5 working days for a faster VOA extension to 12–14 working days for a C1 tourist visa. Each visa above shows its time.

How do I extend my visa?

An extension needs an Indonesian sponsor and a set of application documents. If we handle it, we only need your passport and we prepare everything else.

What happens if I overstay?

Immigration charges a fine of Rp 1,000,000 for every day of overstay. After 60 days of overstay you must pay the fine and you will be deported. Message us as early as you can.

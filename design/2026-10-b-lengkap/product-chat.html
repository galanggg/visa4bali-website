E33G

Remote Worker KITAS, 1 year

A one-year stay permit for people who work for a company outside Indonesia. You can live in Bali, travel in and out, and bring your family.

Stay
1 yearmultiple entry
Processing
12–14 working daysweekends and holidays not counted
Income
US$60,000 a yearor more, from work abroad

Price

Includes the government e-visa fee.

Ask us

We check your documents with you on WhatsApp or Telegram before you pay.

Who it is for

Foreigners who work for a company outside Indonesia and want to live in Bali for a year. The permit lets you stay and bring your family with you.

What we need from you

  • Your passportValid for at least 6 more months.

  • A photo of youA clear photo of the passport holder.

  • Proof of incomeA statement from your company (salary form) or your bank account, showing an income of at least US$60,000 a year.

What the price includes

  • The e-visaPrepared and submitted by our team in Jimbaran.

  • SponsorshipWe act as your sponsor for the application.

  • The government e-visa feePaid by us as part of the application.

How it works

  1. Chat with usTell us about your work and your plans, in English or Russian, on WhatsApp or Telegram.
  2. We check your documentsSend us your passport, photo and proof of income. We confirm the permit fits before you pay.
  3. Pay onlineOnce we confirm the permit fits, you pay on our website.
  4. Receive your KITASWe apply for you and keep you updated until your e-visa arrives by email.

Good to know

Can I bring my family?

Yes, the E33G lets your family come with you. Ask us what each family member needs and what it costs.

Can I work for an Indonesian company with it?

No. The E33G is for people whose employer or clients are outside Indonesia.

Why can't I pay online straight away?

A KITAS depends on your documents. We check them with you first, so you never pay for a permit you cannot get.

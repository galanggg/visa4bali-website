e-VOA

Electronic Visa on Arrival, 30 days

A 30-day Indonesian tourist visa you get before you fly to Bali. You skip the airport VOA queue and go straight to the immigration counter when you land.

Stay
30 daysfrom the day you arrive
Processing
5–7 working daysweekends and holidays not counted
Longest stay
60 dayswith one 30-day extension

Includes the government e-VOA charge.

What we need from you

  • Your passportValid for at least 6 more months. A clear photo of the main page is enough.

  • A photo of youA clear photo of the passport holder.

  • Your onward flight ticketThe ticket out of Indonesia.

What the price includes

  • The e-VOA applicationPrepared and submitted by our team in Jimbaran.

  • The government e-VOA chargeNothing more to pay at the airport for the visa itself.

How it works

  1. Pay onlineApply on this page. You can also message us first if you are unsure.
  2. Send your documentsYour passport photo, your photo and your flight ticket, by WhatsApp or email.
  3. We apply for youWe submit the application and keep you updated on WhatsApp.
  4. Fly to BaliYour e-VOA arrives by email. Show it at the immigration counter when you land.

Good to know

Can I extend the e-VOA?

Yes, once, for another 30 days, so you can stay up to 60 days in total. We handle the VOA extension too.

When should I apply?

Before you fly. Processing takes 5 to 7 working days, and Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are not counted.

I want to stay longer than 60 days

Then the C1 Tourist Visa fits better: 60 days on arrival, extendable to 180 days.

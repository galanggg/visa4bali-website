About us

A visa agency in Jimbaran, Bali, since 2014.

Our story

Visa services from people who have done this for twelve years

Visa4Bali is the trading name of PT Visa Empat Bali. Mr. Rolly started the agency in Jimbaran in 2014 to help foreign visitors with Indonesian visas and extensions, and he still handles difficult stay-permit cases himself.

We take care of each application from start to finish: we collect the documents, prepare the forms, deal with immigration, and bring your passport back. We work in English and Russian.

We prepare the application for you. When something goes wrong with your stay permit, we solve it.

How we work

Direct, transparent, close by

Direct with immigration

We work with immigration officials ourselves, not through brokers. That is why our prices stay clear and predictable.

One person on your case

The person handling your visa keeps you updated on WhatsApp until your passport or e-visa is back with you.

We come to you

Our couriers collect your passport from your hotel or villa and bring it back when the visa is done.
